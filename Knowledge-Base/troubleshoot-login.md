# Troubleshoot Login Issues

| Article ID | KB-004 |
|------------|--------|
| Last Updated | July 2026 |
| Product | Inventory Management System |

## Overview

This article provides solutions for common sign-in issues in the Inventory Management System.

## Common Login Issues

### Invalid Username or Password

If you receive an **Invalid Username or Password** error:

1. Verify that you entered the correct username.
2. Verify that **Caps Lock** is turned off.
3. Re-enter your password.
4. Click **Sign In**.

If the issue continues, reset your password.

---

### Account Locked

Your account may be locked after multiple unsuccessful sign-in attempts.

#### Resolution

1. Wait for 15 minutes.
2. Try signing in again.

If the account remains locked, contact the system administrator.

---

### Password Expired

If your password has expired:

1. Click **Forgot Password**.
2. Complete the password reset process.
3. Sign in using the new password.

---

### Browser Compatibility Issues

If the application does not load correctly:

- Clear your browser cache.
- Refresh the page.
- Use a supported browser:
  - Google Chrome
  - Microsoft Edge
  - Mozilla Firefox

---

### Network Connection Issues

If the sign-in page does not load:

- Verify your internet connection.
- Check whether the application URL is correct.
- Contact your network administrator if the problem persists.
