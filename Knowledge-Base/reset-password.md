# Reset Password

| Article ID | KB-001 |
|------------|--------|
| Last Updated | July 2026 |
| Product | Inventory Management System |

## Overview

This article explains how to reset your password if you are unable to sign in to the Inventory Management System.

## Prerequisites

- You have access to the email address associated with your account.

## Reset Your Password

1. Open the Inventory Management System sign-in page.
2. Click **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Click **Submit**.
5. Open the password reset email.
6. Select the password reset link.
7. Enter a new password.
8. Confirm the new password.
9. Click **Save**.

## Result

Your password is updated successfully. You can now sign in using your new password.

## Troubleshooting

### I did not receive the password reset email.

Verify that:
- The email address is correct.
- The email is not in your Spam or Junk folder.
- The email server is available.

If the issue persists, contact your system administrator.
