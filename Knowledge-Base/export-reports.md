# Export Reports

| Article ID | KB-002 |
|------------|--------|
| Last Updated | July 2026 |
| Product | Inventory Management System |

## Overview

This article explains how to export reports from the Inventory Management System. Users can export reports in PDF and CSV formats for analysis, sharing, and record keeping.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure that:

- You have permission to access the **Reports** module.
- The required report has been generated.
- Pop-ups are enabled in your web browser.

## Export a Report

1. Sign in to the Inventory Management System.
2. Select **Reports** from the navigation menu.
3. Open the report that you want to export.
4. Click **Export**.
5. Select one of the following formats:
   - PDF
   - CSV
6. Click **Download**.

## Result

The system downloads the report to your default download location.

## Notes

- Large reports may take longer to generate.
- Report data reflects the information available at the time of export.
- Exported reports are read-only.

## Troubleshooting

### Export button is unavailable

Verify that:

- The report has finished generating.
- You have permission to export reports.

### The downloaded file cannot be opened

Verify that:

- The download completed successfully.
- The appropriate application is installed to open the selected file format.

If the issue persists, contact your system administrator.
